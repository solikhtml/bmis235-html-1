Music and live entertainment bring community together, it is important to support less known shows to continue getting good art.

Shows to Consider Watching

  • Alice By Heart:Jessie Nelson and Steven Sater Twist on the story of Alice in Wonderland set in World War II. Made by the same creators that made Spring Awakening
  • Parade:Alfred Uhry Tells the true story of the court case following the killing of Mary Fagian in 1913. Covering antisemetism, racism, and misoginy in the deep south
  • Chess:Björn Ulvaeus, Tim Rice, Danny Strong Tells story of chess rivalry in the 1980's between the USSR and the USA, uses that darker synth that differs from the creators other work Mamma Mia
  • Kinky Boots: Harvey Fierstein Follows Charlie Price who inherits his fathers failing shoe company. He teams with a Drag Queen known as Lola to revive the company creating footwear for Drag Queens.
